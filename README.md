# 🚚 SwiftRoute Logistics Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
SwiftRoute Logistics operates a multi-hub delivery network consisting of hubs, drivers, and vehicles responsible for processing and delivering customer orders. This Power BI project provides a centralized analytics solution that enables management to monitor operational performance, identify bottlenecks, improve delivery efficiency, and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Problem
SwiftRoute Logistics lacked a unified reporting system to monitor operational KPIs across its logistics network. Decision-makers needed visibility into:
- Total orders processed
- On-time delivery performance
- Customer satisfaction levels
- Hub capacity utilization
- Driver productivity and delays
- Vehicle utilization and maintenance risks

## Objectives
- Monitor logistics performance in real time
- Track customer satisfaction and delivery efficiency
- Evaluate hub workload and operational capacity
- Identify drivers contributing to delivery delays
- Analyze vehicle utilization and breakdown patterns
- Support strategic planning through actionable insights

## Key Business Insights

### Hub Performance
- High-performing hubs process substantially larger order volumes.
- Capacity utilization varies across hubs.
- Workload balancing opportunities exist.

### Driver Analysis
- Experience generally correlates with stronger ratings.
- A small number of drivers contribute disproportionately to delays.
- Targeted coaching can improve service quality.

### Vehicle Analysis
- Older vehicles show higher breakdown frequencies.
- Vehicle reliability differs by model.
- Preventive maintenance can reduce downtime.

### Customer Experience
- Delivery performance directly impacts customer satisfaction.
- Improving on-time delivery can improve customer retention.

## Business Impact
This dashboard enables management to:
- Improve operational visibility
- Increase on-time delivery performance
- Optimize hub capacity utilization
- Reduce delivery delays
- Improve fleet reliability
- Enhance customer satisfaction
- Support data-driven decisions
