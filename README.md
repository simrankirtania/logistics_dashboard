# 📌 Project Overview

Modern logistics companies process thousands of delivery orders every month across multiple distribution hubs, drivers, and vehicles. Without a centralized reporting solution, monitoring operational efficiency, identifying delivery bottlenecks, and evaluating fleet performance becomes difficult.

This interactive Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive view of logistics operations through intuitive visualizations, allowing stakeholders to monitor key operational KPIs, compare hub performance, evaluate driver productivity, analyze vehicle utilization, and improve overall supply chain efficiency.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Logistics management required a centralized reporting solution capable of answering critical operational questions such as:

- How many orders were delivered during the selected period?
- Are deliveries being completed on time?
- How has delivery performance changed compared to the previous month?
- Which hubs are operating beyond their capacity?
- Which hubs are the best and worst performers?
- Which drivers contribute the most delivery delays?
- Does driver experience impact performance ratings?
- Which vehicles require frequent maintenance?
- Which vehicle models handle the highest order volumes?
- How can fleet utilization and operational efficiency be improved?

The objective was to replace manual operational reporting with an interactive Power BI dashboard that supports faster, smarter, and data-driven business decisions.

---

# 🚀 Solution

Developed a **four-page interactive Power BI dashboard** consisting of:

- Executive Dashboard
- Hubs Overview
- Drivers Overview
- Vehicles Overview

The dashboard enables users to dynamically filter data by **Year**, **Month**, **Hub**, **Driver**, and **Vehicle Status** while monitoring operational KPIs and logistics performance across the organization.

# 💡 Business Insights

Some key insights generated from the dashboard include:

- Delivery performance can be monitored month-over-month using interactive KPI cards.
- Hub capacity analysis helps identify overloaded and underutilized distribution centers.
- High-performing hubs consistently process more orders while maintaining operational efficiency.
- Driver experience generally shows a positive relationship with performance ratings.
- Delay analysis identifies drivers who may require additional coaching or operational support.
- A relatively small percentage of the fleet remains under maintenance, allowing managers to monitor vehicle availability.
- Older vehicles tend to experience more frequent breakdowns, supporting preventive maintenance planning.
- Vehicle model utilization highlights which fleet assets contribute most to delivery operations.
- Dynamic filtering enables quick operational analysis across different months and logistics regions.
